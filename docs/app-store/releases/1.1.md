# Release 1.1 — CarPlay

- **Marketing version:** `1.1`  (`MARKETING_VERSION` in `ios/project.yml`)
- **Build:** `2`  (`CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION`)
- **Headline:** CarPlay support, plus playback reliability fixes.

> ⚠️ **Before you submit 1.1 with these notes:** CarPlay must actually be *in* the build.
> That needs Apple's `com.apple.developer.carplay-audio` entitlement wired in (see
> [`../CARPLAY.md`](../CARPLAY.md)). If the entitlement isn't approved/enabled yet, either
> wait, or ship 1.1 **without** the CarPlay line below (keep only the reliability fixes) —
> Apple rejects "What's New" that advertises a feature the build doesn't have.

---

## What's New — English (paste into the primary localization)

CarPlay is here. Sonalex now works on your car's screen: browse your recent music, playlists, albums and artists, search, and control playback — all from CarPlay, in sync with your phone.

This update also makes playback smoother and more reliable:
• When the connection drops, the music no longer jumps to another song — it keeps your track and picks it up again when you're back online.
• Downloaded songs and offline mode load right away.
• Faster search, quicker track changes, and the next cover is ready before the song even starts.
• Album and artist artwork loads more reliably.

Thanks for using Sonalex.

## Nouveautés — Français (localisation française)

CarPlay est là. Sonalex s'affiche maintenant sur l'écran de ta voiture : tes écoutes récentes, tes playlists, albums et artistes, la recherche et les commandes de lecture — le tout depuis CarPlay, synchronisé avec ton téléphone.

Cette mise à jour rend aussi la lecture plus fluide et plus fiable :
• Quand la connexion saute, la musique ne passe plus à un autre morceau — elle garde ton titre et le reprend dès que le réseau revient.
• Les morceaux téléchargés et le mode hors connexion se chargent immédiatement.
• Recherche plus rapide, changements de morceau plus réactifs, et la pochette suivante est prête avant même le début du son.
• Les pochettes d'albums et d'artistes se chargent plus fiablement.

Merci d'utiliser Sonalex.

## Submit steps for an update (App Store Connect)

1. In App Store Connect → your app → **+ Version or Platform** → enter `1.1`.
2. Paste the "What's New" above into **What's New in This Version** (per localization).
3. Bump is already done in `ios/project.yml` (1.1 / build 2) — run `xcodegen`, then in Xcode
   **Product → Archive** → **Distribute App** → upload.
4. Pick the new build on the version page, keep screenshots/description (or update), **Save**,
   **Add for Review** → **Submit**.
5. Build numbers must always increase. Next time, bump `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` again.
